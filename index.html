About Native Linings

Native Linings is an Indigenous-owned wall and ceiling contractor serving Brisbane, the Sunshine Coast and surrounding areas. We bring hands-on trade experience and practical coordination to commercial, residential and insurance work.

Our focus is straightforward: clear scope, skilled workmanship and a finish the client can rely on.

Our Services

Wall and ceiling linings, from framing through to the finished surface.

Commercial Internal Linings

Wall and ceiling packages for new builds, refurbishments and fit-outs, including offices, retail, healthcare and education spaces.

  • Retail, office & industrial office fit-outs
  • Medical & education facilities
  • Partitions & wall systems
  • Suspended ceilings
  • Fire-rated systems
  • Acoustic systems
  • External soffits
  • CFC cladding

Residential Plastering

Plastering for new homes, renovations and multi-residential builds, including detailed architectural finishes.

  • New homes
  • Renovations & extensions
  • Architectural & high-end homes
  • Unit & townhouse developments
  • Internal wall & ceiling linings
  • Bulkheads, reveals & feature details

Insurance Repair & Reinstatement

Repair and replacement of damaged plasterboard for insurers, builders and property managers.

  • Water, flood & fire damage reinstatement
  • Make-safe works
  • Maintenance, patching & replacement
